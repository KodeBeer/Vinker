**What is it?**

This recursive sound instrument is based on Jaap Vink's technique as explained by Kees Tazelaar in the video:
https://youtu.be/X_Bcr_HS9XM

I also took inspiration from the SuperCollider codes in this this topic on the SuperCollider forum: 
https://scsynth.org/t/jaap-vink-analogue-unstable-ring-modulated-feedback/8613/2


**How to play**

Load this script into the Norns and play it by moving the three encoders

e1= Filter frequency

e2= Ring modulator frequency

e3= Delay time
